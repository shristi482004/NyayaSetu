# User Manual — NyayaSetu

## 1. Introduction to NyayaSetu
**NyayaSetu** is an AI-powered legal document intelligence platform engineered to help ordinary citizens and legal professionals understand complex legal notices, petitions, court orders, and contracts.

> **Important Legal Disclaimer:** NyayaSetu is an informational assistance platform powered by artificial intelligence. The explanations, summaries, flowcharts, and answers generated by the system are provided strictly for educational and informational understanding. They do NOT constitute formal legal advice, representation, or professional counsel. Consult a qualified advocate or attorney for legal matters.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Account Registration and Login
1. Navigate to the registration page (`POST /api/register` or frontend UI in Phase 2).
2. Enter your full name, valid email address, and secure password (minimum 8 characters).
3. Select your intended user role:
   - **Citizen:** Tailored for individuals, consumers, and students seeking clear, simplified explanations without complex legal jargon.
   - **Lawyer:** Tailored for advocates and legal professionals requiring argument mapping, procedural timelines, and case facts.
4. After registration, log in using your credentials to obtain an active session token.

### 2.2 Uploading a Legal Document
1. Supported file format: Digital or scanned **PDF** files up to **25MB**.
2. Submit your document via the upload interface or API (`POST /api/documents`).
3. The platform validates your file and begins text extraction:
   - Digital PDFs are parsed instantly using PyMuPDF.
   - Scanned or photographed documents automatically activate the high-accuracy OCR fallback engine.
4. Once extraction is complete, the document status transitions to `"ready"`.

## 3. Understanding Document Intelligence Modules

### 3.1 Plain-Language Summary
- **Dispute Type:** Identifies the category of legal matter (e.g. Contractual Dispute, Consumer Complaint, Tenancy Notice).
- **Key Parties:** Lists the claimants, respondents, petitioners, and legal entities involved.
- **Factual Background:** Bullet points summarizing essential undisputed facts.
- **Plain-Language Overview:** A direct, non-technical explanation answering: *What is this document about, and why does it matter to me?*

### 3.2 Legal FAQs
- Anticipates the 5 to 8 most urgent questions a citizen would ask (e.g. *Do I need to appear in court?*, *What happens if I ignore this notice?*).
- Answers directly link back to specific sections and clauses in your document.

### 3.3 Procedural Flowcharts
- Displays a visual, step-by-step sequential guide showing what stage the dispute is currently in and what formal legal stages follow next.
- Highlights mandatory statutory deadlines (e.g., *15-day response window*, *30-day appeal limit*).

### 3.4 Chronological Timelines and Action Items
- **Timeline:** Lists every date mentioned in the document and the associated event.
- **Action Items:** Outlines concrete immediate steps categorized by priority (High, Medium, Low), responsible parties, and deadlines.

### 3.5 Argument Maps (Lawyer Persona)
- Breaks down contested assertions into:
  - **Claim:** The specific allegation made by a party.
  - **Evidentiary Basis:** Facts or documentary proof cited in support.
  - **Counter-Argument:** Counter-points or defenses identified in the record.

## 4. Using the Document Chat
- You can ask natural-language questions about your uploaded document at any time.
- **Grounded Answers:** Answers are drawn exclusively from the uploaded document text.
- **Page References:** Citations clearly tell you which page contains the evidence.
- **Handling Unknowns:** If the document does not mention an answer (for instance, asking about unstated dates or off-record claims), NyayaSetu will explicitly state that the information is not present in the document.

## 5. Security and Privacy
- Your documents are private and strictly accessible only by your authenticated account.
- The platform enforces tenant isolation: neither other citizens nor unauthorized personnel can view your uploaded files or chat records.
